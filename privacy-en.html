Privacy Policy — Aurora Flow

Versão em português

Last updated: July 2026

Your data is yours

Aurora Flow does not sell or share your health information. All the health data you log is stored locally on your device. No account is required to use the app.

We collect no data

Aurora Flow collects no usage data. The app contains no analytics, tracking or advertising tools. We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics or any equivalent service. We show no ads and share no information with third parties.

Data you log

Aurora Flow stores the data you voluntarily enter: cycle dates, symptoms, mood, energy and personal notes. This data stays on your device and is not sent to external servers, unless you enable Google Drive backup.

Google Drive backup

Google Drive backup is an optional feature available to premium subscribers. It is off by default. When enabled, your data is uploaded in encrypted form to your own Google Drive account — the Aurora Flow team cannot access those files, and we operate no server that receives your data.

Health data

The health information you log in the app (menstrual cycle, symptoms, mood) is treated as sensitive data and handled with the utmost care. It is stored locally and only leaves your device if you enable backup.

Permissions

The app may request access to your Google account solely for the Drive backup feature. This permission is optional and can be revoked at any time in your device settings.

Premium subscription

Payments are processed by Google Play. Aurora Flow never receives or stores your card or payment details.

Data deletion

You can permanently delete all your data at any time in the app settings. Uninstalling the app also removes all local data from your device. If you enabled Google Drive backup, the backup files remain in your Drive account until you delete them manually.
